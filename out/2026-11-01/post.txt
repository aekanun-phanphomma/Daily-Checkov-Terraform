You apply a key policy with Principal = { AWS = "*" } on a customer master key.

Step 1: An external entity discovers the KMS key ARN through leaked ciphertext metadata.
Step 2: The attacker authenticates with valid credentials from an unrelated AWS account.
Step 3: The attacker calls the kms:Decrypt API against the target key ARN to decipher sensitive data.
Step 4: The attacker issues kms:GenerateDataKey requests to seal tampered artifacts with your key.

CKV_AWS_33 fails any KMS key policy allowing access to wildcard principals without a condition block.

Replace the wildcard in Principal.AWS with a specific account root or IAM role ARN such as arn:aws:iam::123456789012:root.
