Myth: Port 3389 exposed to 0.0.0.0/0 is safe if instances require complex passwords and Network Level Authentication.

Fact: Opening TCP port 3389 to the entire internet allows any remote actor to initiate Remote Desktop Protocol handshakes, attempt credential stuffing, or exploit protocol implementation flaws before credentials are fully verified. Host-level defenses do not prevent denial of service or automated scanning when the network perimeter is wide open.

CKV_AWS_25 fails any security group ingress rule that permits traffic to port 3389 from the 0.0.0.0/0 CIDR block.

Replace 0.0.0.0/0 in the cidr_blocks argument with a specific private subnet such as 10.0.0.0/16 or manage administration through Systems Manager Session Manager.
