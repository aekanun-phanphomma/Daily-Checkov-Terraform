You apply source_address_prefix = "*" on an inbound rule allowing destination port 3389.

Step 1: An internet scanner detects an open TCP socket on port 3389 at your public IP address.
Step 2: The attacker initiates Remote Desktop Protocol handshakes to enumerate local accounts and brute-force credentials.
Step 3: After authenticating into an interactive session, the attacker executes scripts to dump memory and pivot deeper into your virtual network.

Inbound network security rules that permit traffic to port 3389 from any source address are blocked.
Change source_address_prefix to a trusted private CIDR like 10.0.0.0/16.

Would your pipeline catch this inbound security rule before apply?
