Publicly exposing SSH ports invites constant brute-force attacks and vulnerability scanning across your infrastructure.

Checkov check CKV_GCP_2 evaluates google_compute_firewall resources to ensure ingress rules do not open port 22 to the public internet using unrestricted source CIDRs.

When a firewall rule grants unrestricted ingress to port 22, any external actor can communicate directly with your virtual machine instances. Internet-facing SSH services face continuous password spraying and exploitation attempts targeting host access. Restricting ingress access prevents direct scanning and keeps your instances hidden from untrusted networks.

To resolve this issue, replace 0.0.0.0/0 in the source_ranges argument with trusted management subnets or the Google Cloud Identity-Aware Proxy range 35.235.240.0/20.
