Exposing port 22 directly to the public internet invites automated reconnaissance and persistent brute-force attacks against administrative interfaces.

Checkov check CKV_AWS_24 evaluates AWS security groups to ensure they do not permit inbound network traffic to TCP port 22 from the public internet address 0.0.0.0/0.

Leaving SSH open to all IP addresses allows any unauthorized actor on the internet to attempt credential stuffing or exploit potential OpenSSH daemon vulnerabilities. If an instance accepts connections indiscriminately, an attacker only needs compromised credentials or an unpatched flaw to obtain an interactive shell and move laterally through your cloud environment. Restricting administrative access to private ranges shields instances from automated internet-wide scanning while enforcing access control through dedicated bastions, corporate VPNs, or AWS Systems Manager.

To resolve this issue, update the ingress rule by setting cidr_blocks to a trusted internal network such as 10.0.0.0/16 or specific administrator IP addresses instead of 0.0.0.0/0.
