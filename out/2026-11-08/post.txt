Can a custom Azure role include wildcard actions without being flagged as a subscription owner?

The evaluation inspects azurerm_role_definition resources by examining the first permissions block and its actions list argument. If the actions list contains a wildcard asterisk, the resource fails the check. If the actions list contains explicit resource provider operations and excludes the wildcard, the resource passes.

What surprises many engineers is that the scanner flags any presence of the asterisk in actions regardless of what you specify elsewhere. Even if you declare restricted scopes or attempt to carve out sensitive operations using not_actions, containing a wildcard in actions still triggers a failure.

Broad permissions grant blanket administrative control across the subscription management plane. Any compromised identity assigned this role definition can invoke any control plane API, modify critical resources, or bypass organizational governance policies.

Replace the asterisk wildcard in the permissions.actions argument with explicit operations such as Microsoft.Resources/subscriptions/read.
